\section*{Mission}
MMM is a controlled discovery, simulation, fabrication, replication, and validation platform for testing whether engineered metamaterial environments measurably alter decoherence mechanisms in quantum devices.

\section*{Baseline-first doctrine}
The baseline mechanism hierarchy is:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item vortex loss
    \item quasiparticle poisoning
    \item two-level systems
    \item phonon coupling
    \item control electronics noise
\end{enumerate}
No metamaterial claim is promotable until these explanations are quantitatively attacked.

\section*{Lead branches}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Photonic-bandgap / electromagnetic-LDOS suppression
    \item Phononic-bandgap engineering of TLS relaxation
    \item Phonon escape routing and down-conversion
    \item Vortex-pinning lattice and parked-vortex control (baseline-null branch)
\end{itemize}

\section*{Governance}
Every mechanism entry in the registry references:
\begin{itemize}
    \item predicted observable IDs
    \item experimental protocol IDs
    \item validation rule IDs
\end{itemize}
Mechanisms lacking falsification pathways are rejected. Exotic fabrication is flagged and blocked from lead-branch promotion unless mitigated.
