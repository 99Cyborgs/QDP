## NON NEGOTIABLE RULES

R1. Null model priority.  
Construct and test the minimal physically justified Lindblad baseline first.

R2. Observable anchoring.  
Every claim must tie to measured or directly measurable observables.

R3. Joint fit discipline.  
Fit all available experiments jointly. Independent curve fitting followed by narrative reconciliation is forbidden.

R4. Residual primacy.  
Judge adequacy by residual structure on time series observables, not fit score alone.

R5. Drift realism.  
If data span multiple runs, days, or long traces, include a hierarchical drift model and log drift structure explicitly.

R6. Identifiability requirement.  
A Hamiltonian term survives only if its signatures cannot be reproduced by known mechanisms, artifacts, nuisance models, or Lindblad equivalent formulations within measurement resolution.

R7. Orthogonal axis requirement.  
No candidate may survive on one perturbation axis alone. At least two orthogonal discriminant axes are required, and one must separate the candidate from the strongest competing mechanism.

R8. Parameter economy.  
If more than two unconstrained new parameters are introduced at once, default promotion is capped at SANDBOX_ONLY unless independent constraints are explicitly provided.

R9. Known limit preservation.  
Every extension must recover the baseline model in a clearly defined reduction limit.

R10. Artifact discipline.  
Residual structure is not evidence of new physics until calibration, readout, control chain, and environmental systematic routes are stress tested.

R11. Geometry discipline.  
Geometry scaling is interpretable only if fabrication, material stack, and process are held fixed or explicit confounds are carried forward.

R12. Numerical credibility.  
Reject any conclusion that changes qualitatively under timestep, truncation, solver, or sampling refinement.

R13. Registry memory.  
Never discard rejected candidates. Preserve negative results, tested ranges, anomalies, failure modes, falsifiers, evidence records, dataset links, calibration status, and experiment failures.

R14. Hard gate logic.  
No later stage may execute after an earlier terminal rejection.

R15. No generic rejected state.  
Use only the explicit scientific decision enum.

R16. Cross-device promotion discipline.  
No surviving candidate may receive PROCEED without cross-device confirmation or an explicit rejection state that makes cross-device testing irrelevant.

R17. Anomaly before attribution.  
Any structured residual that survives baseline fitting must be registered before mechanism assignment or Hamiltonian interpretation.

R18. Anomaly persistence rule.  
Unexplained residual structure may justify SANDBOX_ONLY exploration, but never PROCEED, until discriminant experiments reduce ambiguity.

R19. Registry federation supremacy.  
Candidate registry, falsifier registry, anomaly ledger, family statistics, promotion history, evidence ledger, experiment ledger, dataset ledger, drift ledger, and failure mode library must resolve through one canonical governance object.

R20. Duplicate by behavior rule.  
A candidate may count as duplicate even if symbolic form differs, if claimed effect, falsifier, observable set, and effective dissipative behavior match prior entries within measurement resolution.

R21. Falsifier inheritance rule.  
If a branch is a near duplicate of a prior family entry, inherited falsifiers must be carried forward unless explicit novelty invalidates them.

R22. Promotion history irreversibility.  
Once a family or candidate is capped downward by unresolved anomaly, weak falsifiable surface area, poor identifiability, weak evidence quality, weak experiment design, or low quantitative score, later stages may not silently restore full promotion rights.

R23. Claim magnitude scaling rule.  
Required evidence strength must scale with the magnitude and generality of the claim.

R24. Confidence law.  
Every stage must emit confidence and evidence quality, not just pass or fail language.

R25. Mixed evidence penalty.  
Contradictory evidence across stages lowers effective confidence even if no single stage is terminal.

R26. No confidence laundering.  
Low quality evidence cannot be aggregated into high confidence by repetition alone.

R27. Experimental executability rule.  
No discriminant experiment may be counted unless control variables, observables, kill criteria, required bandwidth, and calibration conditions are explicitly stated.

R28. Minimal discriminant rule.  
The preferred experiment is the smallest protocol that breaks degeneracy between the candidate and the strongest competing mechanism.

R29. Control pair requirement.  
Whenever possible, experiments must include a matched control pair, matched device pair, matched field history pair, or matched line configuration pair.

R30. Evidence uplift requirement.  
An experiment schedule must specify what evidence class or confidence increase it is expected to deliver.

R31. Underpowered design penalty.  
If a proposed experiment lacks sufficient device count, protocol contrast, fabrication control, or observable sensitivity to discriminate mechanisms, it may not raise governance status.

R32. Dataset provenance rule.  
No dataset may be treated as independent evidence unless device lineage, fabrication batch, calibration context, and protocol metadata are recorded.

R33. Calibration integrity rule.  
No residual interpretation may proceed if calibration validity is below threshold.

R34. Instrument realism rule.  
Experiments that demand bandwidth, timing resolution, or readout fidelity beyond declared instrument capability are non executable.

R35. Novelty burden rule.  
Symbolic variation without observable, falsifier, invariant, or mechanism separation novelty counts against the branch.

R36. Quantitative decision rule.  
Final governance is constrained both by logical gates and by machine weighted stage scoring.

R37. Replication forecast rule.  
A branch may not receive PROCEED if an independent lab replication forecast is implausible.

R38. Aging and archive rule.  
Stagnant branches with repeated failed attempts and no evidence uplift must be archived.

## CLAIM MAGNITUDE AND BURDEN LAW

LOCAL_BRANCH

MECHANISM_LEVEL  
Claim asserts a distinct physical mechanism explains residual structure.

DEVICE_CLASS_LEVEL  
Claim asserts generality across a device class or geometry class.

PLATFORM_LEVEL  
Claim asserts generality across material stacks, fabrication families, or multiple hardware platforms.

FOUNDATIONAL_PHYSICS  
Claim asserts implications beyond established decoherence mechanism hierarchy or implies new underlying physics.

Burden mapping:

LOCAL_BRANCH -> MINIMAL or STANDARD

higher evidence quality  
more orthogonal discriminants  
more independent falsifier survival  
stronger cross device support  
lower anomaly burden  
higher numerical stability confidence  
stronger experiment design quality

No FOUNDATIONAL_PHYSICS claim may survive with only single device evidence.

DESIGN SUFFICIENCY RULES

An experiment is design sufficient only if:

the observable is sensitive to the proposed mechanism difference  
the perturbation axis is orthogonal to at least one previously used axis  
the expected signal exceeds the noise floor or bandwidth floor in principle  
required calibration controls are stated  
required device count is stated  
required repeat count is stated  
failure interpretation is stated

DESIGN PENALTY RULES

If required_device_count is unspecified, the experiment cannot raise governance status.  
If kill_criterion is missing, the experiment is invalid.  
If success_criterion only states fit improvement, the experiment is invalid.  
If matched_control_description is absent where feasible, evidence quality is capped at MODERATE.

## STAGE DEFINITIONS

### STAGE 0 — TAXONOMY CLASSIFICATION
Infer assigned_family_class from candidate_H_mod_symbolic. Compare against declared_family_class. Record mismatch. Record family_status. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 1 — FEDERATED REGISTRY LOOKUP
Check exact duplicates, algebraic duplicates, effective Lindblad duplicates, family near duplicates, behavioral duplicates, inherited falsifiers, prior anomaly overlap, prior failure modes, and branch aging history. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 2 — FORK REGISTRATION VALIDATION
Validate required intake fields using fork discipline and automatic flags. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 3 — INPUT AND DATASET VALIDATION
Validate units, time sampling, bandwidth, metadata completeness, dataset provenance, calibration chain completeness, and data quality.

If dataset governance fails, defer or reject depending on severity.

Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 4 — IDENTIFIABILITY SCREEN AND BURDEN ASSIGNMENT
Assess orthogonal perturbation axes and assign claim_magnitude and burden_level. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 4A — CALIBRATION INTEGRITY GATE
Compute calibration_validity_score. If below 70, defer and terminate. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 5 — BASELINE MODEL
Construct H0_BASELINE_LINDBLAD with joint fitting, explicit noise model, trace preservation, complete positivity, and reduction to textbook limit.

If the baseline pipeline executes successfully:
  L0_baseline_pipeline_reproduced = true

Emit evidence entries. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 6 — RESIDUAL DIAGNOSTICS
Analyze residual structure on raw observables.

If residuals are white, stationary, structurally absent, and cross observable correlations vanish after drift aware fitting:
  scientific_decision = REJECTED_BY_BASELINE_SUFFICIENCY
  governance_outcome = REJECT
  cross_device_status = NOT_REQUIRED
  append result to gate_trace
  terminate

Emit evidence entries. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 6A — ANOMALY LEDGER REGISTRATION
Register every surviving structured residual as anomaly before attribution. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 6B — DRIFT LEDGER UPDATE
Log detected drift and nonstationarity explicitly. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 7 — MECHANISM PRIOR AND SIGNATURE MATCHING
Compute priors and map signatures to likely bath classes using the signature chart and glossary. Emit evidence entries. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 8 — CLASSICAL MECHANISM TESTING
Test vortex, quasiparticle, TLS, phonon, control noise, EM Purcell, and parsimonious combinations.

If any known mechanism or parsimonious combination explains the data:
  scientific_decision = REJECTED_BY_CLASSICAL_MECHANISM_EQUIVALENCE
  governance_outcome = REJECT
  cross_device_status = NOT_REQUIRED
  append result to gate_trace
  terminate

Emit evidence entries. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 9 — ARTIFACT ELIMINATION
Stress test control chain, calibration, readout, timing, line reflections, AWG or IQ imbalance, and cryocooler synchronized artifacts.

If artifact route explains the data:
  scientific_decision = REJECTED_BY_ARTIFACT_EQUIVALENCE
  governance_outcome = REJECT
  cross_device_status = NOT_REQUIRED
  append result to gate_trace
  terminate

Emit evidence entries. Append result to gate_trace.

### STAGE 10 — HAMILTONIAN TESTING
Define H = H0 + H_mod only after prior stages survive.

If convergence plan is declared before nonlinear sweeps:
  L2_convergence_plan_defined = true
Else:
  scientific_decision = NOT_EVALUATED
  governance_outcome = DEFER
  cross_device_status = NOT_REQUIRED
  append result to gate_trace
  terminate

Verify numerically that H_mod -> 0 recovers H0 within tolerance.
If verified:
  L1_reduction_limit_verified = true
Else:
  scientific_decision = NOT_EVALUATED
  governance_outcome = REJECT
  cross_device_status = NOT_REQUIRED
  append result to gate_trace
  terminate

Compute joint predictions and orthogonal observables.
Emit evidence entries. Append result to gate_trace.

## DECISION DISCIPLINE

Interpretation is subordinate to falsification.

Residual structure is evidence of model insufficiency, not evidence of new physics.

A surviving anomaly justifies more disciplined experiments, not narrative escalation.

A near duplicate is not novelty because its symbol changed.

Weak evidence does not become strong because it is repeated in more paragraphs.

A pretty experiment schedule is worthless if it cannot break degeneracy.

A high fit score with low identifiability is still weak science.
