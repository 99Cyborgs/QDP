# QDP MASTER SPEC

## Purpose

QDP is the execution project for disciplined modeling of quantum decoherence with experimentally testable structure.

OCH remains the upstream theoretical archive.

QDP is constrained to:

• Nonlinear Hamiltonian development  
• Open system numerical modeling  
• Validation harness design  
• Parameter discipline  
• Falsifier tracking  
• Instrument facing prediction  

Cosmological narrative, symbolic analogy, and interpretive synthesis are excluded from evidentiary status.

---

## Scope Lock

In scope:

• Nonlinear Hamiltonian modification  
• Low dimensional tractable systems  
• Explicit invariants and reduction limits  
• Convergence tested numerics  
• One primary observable and one secondary observable  
• Registered falsifiers  

Out of scope:

• Macro cosmological fitting  
• Universal claims  
• Parameter inflation without independent constraint  
• Cross scale extrapolation without effective mapping  

---

## Operating Structure

### Governance Triad

Order  
Specification, baselines, reproducibility, version control.

Change  
Sandbox exploration, controlled parameter sweeps.

Harmony  
Validation, pruning, model selection.

Order constrains Change. Harmony selects survivors.

---

## Validation Ladder

Level 0 Baseline reproduction  
Level 1 Reduction limit recovery  
Level 2 Numerical convergence  
Level 3 Observable stability  
Level 4 Instrument facing comparison  

No branch escalates without passing lower levels.

---

## Escalation Rule

Tier 2 requires at least one finalized converged run artifact.

Tier escalation must increase falsifiable surface area.

---

## Focus Statement

QDP tests whether nonlinear Hamiltonian modifications generate measurable decoherence relevant behavior while preserving reduction to known limits.

This focus is binding.
